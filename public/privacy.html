Privacy

Your plan stays under your control.

MacroForge does not require an account. Planner inputs, saved check-ins and workout completion data are stored locally in your browser.

Information stored by the planner

Your age, sex, height, weight, planning preferences, generated-plan state, check-ins and workout completion state may be saved using browser local storage. MacroForge does not send these values to an application database.

PDF downloads and payment

The free basic PDF is generated in your browser and contains only your daily calorie target. Full PDF export costs ₹9 for the plan snapshot at checkout. After payment is verified, that snapshot is sent to a Cloudflare Worker to generate the full report in memory; MacroForge does not save it in a database or log its contents. A signed, HttpOnly browser cookie links the payment to your purchased snapshot for 24 hours. Keep the same browser open and download your copy before clearing browser data. Razorpay keeps payment information under its own privacy policy.

Deleting your data

Use your browser’s site-data controls to remove data stored for macroforge.site. Clearing browser data or using a different browser or device also removes access to the locally stored history.

External services

The site loads web fonts from Google Fonts and is delivered through Cloudflare. Contribution buttons and paid PDF checkout open Razorpay in a separate window, where Razorpay’s own privacy terms apply. Razorpay retains payment records. MacroForge sends Razorpay only a checkout reference and a one-way plan digest, not your profile or plan. Links to research and source material open third-party websites.

Health information

MacroForge provides educational estimates for generally healthy adults. It is not medical advice and should not be used for pregnancy, breastfeeding, eating-disorder treatment or medical nutrition management without an appropriately qualified clinician.
